Health360: Comprehensive Healthcare Risk & Disease Analytics

The growing prevalence of chronic diseases such as Heart disease, Diabetes, and Stroke has created a major challenge for healthcare providers in identifying high-risk patients at an early stage. This problem matters because delayed detection and poor understanding of health risk factors can lead to severe medical complications, increased treatment costs, and reduced patient outcomes.

To address this, it is important to analyze patient demographic details, clinical indicators, and lifestyle behaviors such as age, BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, glucose levels, smoking habits, alcohol consumption, fatigue conditions, chest pain symptoms, dizziness patterns, and exercise levels to identify disease trends and health risk correlations. Understanding how these factors interact can help reveal hidden patterns and highlight the most influential contributors to chronic disease development.

Additionally, analyzing disease prevalence across different age groups and BMI categories can provide deeper insights into vulnerable patient segments. The expected outcome of this analysis is to generate actionable insights through Tableau dashboards that enable healthcare professionals to detect high-risk individuals, monitor patient health trends, improve preventive care strategies, optimize clinical decision-making, and allocate healthcare resources more effectively.

Ultimately, this project aims to support proactive healthcare management, reduce disease progression, improve treatment planning, and enhance overall patient health outcomes.
